## Problem Statement

For over 20 years, our client has maintained customer accounts using pen and notebooks. Previous bookkeeping software proved too complicated and inefficient during rush hours.

Customers either pay immediately or settle their dues at the end of the month. Transactions are recorded manually, and accounts are reconciled every Sunday, consuming hours of work.

**Objective:** Build a simple, searchable digital notebook that follows the client's existing workflow while making bookkeeping faster and easier.
